🛍️ Retail Sales & Supply Chain Analytics Dashboard

📌 Project Overview - 
An interactive Retail Sales & Supply Chain Analytics Dashboard developed using Power BI to analyze sales performance, profitability, discounts, customer behavior, and product categories. The project transforms raw retail data into meaningful business insights through interactive dashboards and visualizations.

🎯 Project Objectives - 
Analyze total sales and profit performance
Track average sales and profit margins
Understand the impact of discounts on profitability
Identify repeated customers and customer purchasing behavior
Compare sales performance across categories, sub-categories, regions, and time periods
Build an interactive dashboard for data-driven decision maki

🛠️ Tools & Technologies - 
Power BI – Data visualization & dashboard development
Microsoft Excel – Data cleaning and preparation
Power Query – Data transformation
DAX – Calculations and measures

📊 Dashboard Highlights - 
The dashboard includes:
💰 Total Sales
📈 Total Profit
📊 Average Sales
💹 Profit Margin
🏷️ Discount Amount
👥 Repeated Customers
🗂️ Category & Sub-Category Analysis
🌍 Regional Sales Analysis
📅 Year & Month Analysis
🎛️ Interactive Slicers & Filters
🔄 Reset/Interactive dashboard controls

🔍 Key Insights - 
The analysis helps identify sales trends, profitable and less-profitable product segments, the relationship between discounts and profit, and customer purchasing patterns. These insights can help businesses improve pricing strategies, understand customer behavior, and make better inventory and sales decisions.
